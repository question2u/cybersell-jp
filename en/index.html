01 · Business

What we do

Our own brand, and market entry for overseas brands. Both run on the same playbook.

  1. Own brand
    ArtGateHousehold, stationery, storage

    We import household goods made by partner factories overseas and sell them under our own brand, ArtGate, on Amazon.co.jp, TikTok Shop, and Qoo10. Customs clearance, inventory, product pages, and advertising are all handled in-house.

  2. Partners
    E-commerce operationsAmazon, Rakuten, TikTok Shop

    For overseas brands entering Japan, we handle listing, Japanese product pages, advertising, and FBA inbound logistics, using the same process and tools we run for ArtGate.

  3. Partners
    Distribution in JapanWholesale or consignment

    Under an authorized license, we sell overseas brands in Japan on a wholesale or consignment basis, and work with each brand on channel selection and pricing.

02 · Principles

How we work

  1. 1
    Stay compliantWe pay import duties and consumption tax in full, and check product regulations such as PSE before we sell.
  2. 2
    Choose on qualityWe don't carry products likely to cause returns or complaints, even when the price works.
  3. 3
    Keep stock movingWe only buy what we expect to sell through, and we weigh our partners' inventory risk as our own.
  4. 4
    Run on numbersWe review ads, inventory, and sales daily, and share the reasoning behind decisions with partners.

03 · Message

From our representative

I founded Cybersell in 2023 to bring good products from overseas to Japanese homes, in a form that fits how people here live.

Before that, I worked on e-commerce for Geberit, the Swiss plumbing manufacturer, in the Chinese market, learning what an international brand's quality standards demand and how to present them honestly online. I also run an e-commerce business in China. Having seen both the factory floor and the storefront is what this company is built on.

What we care about is simple: follow the law, pay our taxes properly, and deliver the quality we promise, every time. It isn't flashy, but we want to be a company our partners can rely on for years.

Representative Director

Weilu (Robert) Xu

Studied management at China Europe International Business School (CEIBS). Worked on Geberit's e-commerce business in China before founding Cybersell in 2023.
